# Management Summary

## What should GEMA management know?

GEMA's 2026 competition registrations are performing strongly compared with the same period last year. From 1 January to 4 September, registrations increased from **1,800 in 2025 to 2,150 in 2026**, representing **19.4% year-over-year growth**.

### Key Observations
1. **All eight competitions are ahead of last year's pace.** This indicates broad-based growth rather than performance being driven by only one competition.
2. **Student Speaker Competition is the fastest-growing competition at +32.9%**, followed by **International Coding Olympiad at +26.1%** and **International Science Olympiad at +25.1%**.
3. **AI & Robotics Challenge has the slowest growth at +10.0%.** It is still ahead of 2025, but its growth rate is materially below the overall portfolio growth of 19.4%.
4. **July is the only month below last year's level**, with registrations down approximately **4.8% YoY**. Management should review campaign timing, school holidays, and outreach activity during this period.
5. **Recent momentum is strong.** Registrations in the latest four-week period are approximately **31.9% above** the equivalent period in 2025.

### Recommendations
- Maintain promotional focus on the fastest-growing competitions while capacity and demand remain strong.
- Review the July slowdown to understand whether it was seasonal or campaign-related.
- Give additional marketing attention to slower-growing competitions such as AI & Robotics Challenge.
- Continue weekly monitoring so any decline in registration pace can be identified early.
